# Seminars

## Nonequilibrium Quantum Theory Seminar

### November 13, 2026 (Friday)

**Speaker:** 榊 朋央 (Second-year master's student, Theoretical Condensed Matter Physics Group, Graduate School of Science, The University of Osaka)

**Title:** Finite-time error bounds for the Gaussian approximation in a central spin model with intrabath interactions

**Abstract:**

The Gaussian approximation, which describes environmental effects using cumulants up to second order, has been widely used in open quantum systems. However, rigorous justifications have been limited to systems coupled to many independent baths [1] or cases where the environmental operators are static [2], and quantitative errors in the presence of intrabath interactions have not been investigated. In this talk, we report a rigorous finite-time upper bound on the error of the Gaussian approximation for a pure-dephasing model consisting of a single qubit linearly coupled to a one-dimensional spin bath with short-range interactions at infinite temperature. We also show that, when the system–bath coupling weights are distributed over many sites rather than concentrated in a particular region, the error approaches zero while finite decoherence is maintained. Furthermore, we discuss how the time window over which the approximation is guaranteed grows with the number of coupling sites. These results provide quantitative criteria for when a spin bath with intrabath interactions can be regarded as a Gaussian bath.

[1] M. Fantechi and M. Merkli, Phys. Rev. Lett. 136, 060402 (2026).

[2] K. I. O. Ben Attar and N. Bar-Gill, arXiv:2409.12749.

### November 9, 2026 (Monday)

**Speaker:** Kazuki Yamamoto (Assistant Professor, Condensed Matter Theory Group, Graduate School of Science, Kyoto University)

**Title:** Exceptional Majorana boson in bosonic superfluid

**Abstract:**

Majorana fermions have attracted considerable attention in condensed matter physics for many years. They appear in topological superconductors, and their non-Abelian statistics are expected to have applications in topological quantum computation. In contrast, their bosonic counterpart, the Majorana boson, remains largely unexplored. In this talk, we propose the concept of a Majorana boson characterized by a global exceptional point. We show that Majorana bosons emerge at critical points of certain phase transitions in Bose–Einstein condensates (BECs). Specifically, we demonstrate their emergence in two distinct systems: a ferroelectric transition in a magnon BEC [1] and a nonequilibrium BEC transition in a bosonic Kitaev chain [2]. These results suggest that the concept of Majorana bosons may extend beyond particular physical systems and apply to a wide range of systems.

[1] Kazuki Yamamoto, Takuto Kawakami, Mikito Koshino, Phys. Rev. B **114**, 034501 (2026).  
[2] Kazuki Yamamoto, Youichi Yanase, arXiv: 2609.29082 (2026).
