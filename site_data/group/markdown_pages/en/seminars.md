# Seminars

## Nonequilibrium Quantum Theory Seminar

### November 9, 2026 (Monday)

**Speaker:** Kazuki Yamamoto (Assistant Professor, Condensed Matter Theory Group, Graduate School of Science, Kyoto University)

**Title:** Exceptional Majorana boson in bosonic superfluid

**Abstract:**

Majorana fermions have attracted considerable attention in condensed matter physics for many years. They appear in topological superconductors, and their non-Abelian statistics are expected to have applications in topological quantum computation. In contrast, their bosonic counterpart, the Majorana boson, remains largely unexplored. In this talk, we propose the concept of a Majorana boson characterized by a global exceptional point. We show that Majorana bosons emerge at critical points of certain phase transitions in Bose–Einstein condensates (BECs). Specifically, we demonstrate their emergence in two distinct systems: a ferroelectric transition in a magnon BEC [1] and a nonequilibrium BEC transition in a bosonic Kitaev chain [2]. These results suggest that the concept of Majorana bosons may extend beyond particular physical systems and apply to a wide range of systems.

[1] Kazuki Yamamoto, Takuto Kawakami, Mikito Koshino, Phys. Rev. B **114**, 034501 (2026).  
[2] Kazuki Yamamoto, Youichi Yanase, arXiv: 2609.29082 (2026).
